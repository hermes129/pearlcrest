(FAQ)

Common Questions

( 01 )
What types of properties do you offer?
We offer three categories: Off-Plan (pre-launch and under-construction), Developed (ready to move in), and Pre-Owned (secondary market resales). Each comes with its own investment profile and advisory approach.
( 02 )
Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?
Yes. Dubai allows 100% foreign ownership in designated freehold zones — which include most of the city's prime residential areas. Our advisors will confirm eligibility based on your preferred location.
( 03 )
How do I schedule a property viewing?
Fill in the form below or reach us directly. We offer in-person viewings and virtual walkthroughs — whichever suits your schedule and location.
( 04 )
Do you offer mortgage or financing advice?
Yes. We work with approved mortgage brokers and can connect you to financing options for both residents and non-residents. We'll help you understand eligibility, LTV ratios, and approval timelines.
( 05 )
Are there fees on top of the property price?
Standard Dubai transaction fees include 4% DLD transfer tax, a 2% agency fee, and admin charges. We break all of this down before you commit — no surprises at the finish line.
